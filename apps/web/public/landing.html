      How a lot works

      Solana holds the seal before the sale and the full record after it. Everything in between runs inside MagicBlock's private rollup, where nobody can read the contents.

      1. 1

        Sealed on Solana

        The lot is created on Solana with a sha256 seal over every token slot, before any clock exists.

      2. 2

        Into the private rollup

        Lot and seal move into MagicBlock's Private Ephemeral Rollup, an Intel TDX enclave. The seal has no readers, operator included.

      3. 3

        A seed from inside

        The lot asks for a VRF seed from inside the rollup. It lands in the private seal in ~221 ms and decides which facts come out.

      4. 4

        A 10 ms clock

        The price is set by the rollup block. At 15, 30, 45, 60 and 75% of the clock the enclave discloses the next fact, computed with live oracle prices.

      5. 5

        TAKE pays that block's price

        The first TAKE pays the clock price of the block it lands in. Payment and title move in one rollup transaction.

      6. 6

        Opened, re-derivable on Solana

        Contents, salt and seed go public, the tokens go to the buyer, and the record returns to Solana for anyone to re-derive.

      Why a private rollup

      On a plain Solana program this sale can't work: the contents are public, the clock is slow, and the randomness is visible the moment it lands.

      Verify it yourself

      verify reads a lot's final record from Solana and re-derives it from scratch. No wallet, no account.

      • The seal: sha256 over the lot, salt, mints and amounts, against the commitment posted before the clock opened.
      • Every fact: from the seed, the contents and the oracle prices it was computed with.
      • The price paid: from the clock at the block the TAKE landed in.
      • The value: from the amounts and the oracle prices at opening.
      Open Verify in the app
      $npx penlight verify 5
      Lot #5 read from Solana devnet (settled record)
      ✓lot is openedcontents, salt and seed are public
      ✓seal matches contentssha256(lot‖salt‖mints‖amounts) = sealed
      ✓fact 1 re-derivedsame seed, same fact; true for the contents
      ✓fact 2 re-derivedsame seed, same fact; true for the contents
      ✓fact 3 re-derivedsame seed, same fact; true for the contents
      ✓paid the clock priceprice at the TAKE's rollup slot = price paid
      ✓value re-computedΣ amount × oracle price = recorded value
      All 7 checks pass. Seal, facts, price and value all re-derive from the public record.
